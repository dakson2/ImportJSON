## Anonymized account cases

The posting asks for one or two anonymized account cases. Both below are described by category and scope
rather than by client name, and neither carries a performance figure that the source material does not
support. Where the outcome is not documented, it is not claimed.

### Case 1 — Large DTC retailer, strictly regulated consumer category

**Scale.** Google Ads at approximately €2.5M per month, personally managed and optimized. Part of a wider
international portfolio above €5M per month across EMEA and APAC, with more than 15 outsourced agency PPC
specialists coordinated.

**Account scope.** Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display and YouTube. Account structure and
restructuring, search-intent analysis, negative-keyword and search-term governance, feed and Merchant Center
quality, bidding and budget pacing across markets, conversion tracking and attribution, and a reporting
framework built to hold ROAS and CPA decisions.

**What made it difficult, and relevant here.** The category is subject to strict platform policy: creative is
constrained, landing pages are constrained, and disapprovals are a routine operating risk rather than an
exception. Scaling spend under those conditions means the account cannot be grown by simply widening
targeting and creative. Growth has to come from structure, intent quality, feed hygiene and measurement you
can actually trust — which is also what holds efficiency when a DTC account gets large.

**Not claimed:** no ROAS, CPA or revenue delta is stated here, because the source material documents the scope
and scale of the work rather than a specific published outcome. Happy to talk through specifics under NDA.

### Case 2 — Rent-a-car operator, Dubrovnik market

**Why include a much smaller account.** Because it shows a judgement call rather than a budget number.

Campaign performance in that account did not reveal an advertising problem. It revealed an inventory one: the
demand the campaigns were generating exceeded what the fleet could actually service. The correct
recommendation was not to push more spend into the channel but to say so — that the constraint had moved
outside the ad account and that spending against it would have produced bookings the business could not
fulfil.

That is the habit I would bring to a senior buying seat: reading the account as a business signal, and being
willing to recommend against spend when the data says the bottleneck is elsewhere. The result is deliberately
stated without inflation.
